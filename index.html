Highlights

Victory At King's Cup Bangkok, Thailand

    In 2019, I reached a major milestone in my squash career by winning the prestigious King’s Cup in Bangkok, Thailand. Competing on an international stage against top-tier players from across the region was a massive challenge, but standing on the podium made the hard work worth it. This victory remains one of my proudest moments. It solidified my place as an international competitor and fueled my passion to push even harder as a SUKMA athlete and bring passion to the players I coach.
