Highlights

Gold at SUKMA 2026

  • This year, one of my students won gold at SUKMA 2026, and watching them win on the biggest stage was a proud moment for me. Most of the work happened in training, long before the final. The early mornings, the repeated drills and the sessions where nothing seemed to click all added up to that day. Seeing it pay off meant a lot, both for the player and for me as their coach. SUKMA is one of the toughest tournaments in the country, and standing next to them as they took the podium made all of it worth it. As a coach, nothing beats seeing a player I’ve worked with reach that level, and it makes me want to keep pushing the next group of players even harder.
