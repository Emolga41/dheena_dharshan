Profile

Dheena Dharshan Sivabalan

  • I am a Malaysian-born international squash player, SUKMA athlete and profound coach who aims to accelarate skill development and foster growth in the squash industry.
  • Alongside my squash career, I hold a Bachelor's Degree in the field of Business from INTI International College, Subang and am currently serving as a Project & Service Executive at Midas Utara Engineering Sdn Bhd.
  • I bring passion to the court and inspire players across Malaysia to instill discipline, sportsmanship and a positive attitude.

Qualifications
Player

As A Squash Player

  • Consistent Top-3 Finisher: Maintained a top 3 ranking in both national and international tournaments through disciplined performance.
  • 4-Year Selangor SUKMA Representative: Proudly represented Selangor for four consecutive years at the prestigious Sukan Malaysia Games.
  • National Squash7 Champion: Secured 1st place in the Men's Squash7 category at the OMESTI Healthcare 37th National Squash Championships.
Coach

As A Squash Coach

  • 11+ Years of National Coaching Experience: Accelerated technique, movement, and match fitness for over 100 players across the country.
  • Level 3 WSF Certification: Certified at the highest international standard by the World Squash Federation, with a focus on advanced technique, strategy and consistency.
  • Founder of HotShot Squash Academy: Partnered with Next Level Squash to deliver high-impact training programs that inspire players and drive competitive success.
  • Comprehensive Player Development: Delivering personalized training tailored to every stage, experience in developing both beginners and advanced players.
